Welcome!

My name is Scott and I'm a professional organizer here in Michigan! I recently graduated from Miami University, and during my time there, I saw so many messy rooms! Although a typical guest would just ignore it, I always found myself asking one question: "Can I organize your room?" After organizing so many rooms for fellow students, it hit me: What if I could do this as a career? That's when I decided to create Scott Organizing, my vision for exactly what a professional organizing service should be!

What is professional organizing?

Let's be real: a lot of people have messy living spaces. It's no surprise, considering tons of people are either way too busy or have things that prevent them from keeping their spaces organized. That's when it's time to call a professional organizer like me. I'll get your home totally organized so it's way better to live in and enjoy.
