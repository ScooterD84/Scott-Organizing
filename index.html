What is Professional Organizing?

A person skilled in organizing transforms a disorganized living space into an organized one so the person living there can use it daily and easily maintain it.

Some people prefer the transformation to happen all at once, while others prefer it to happen slowly over time. I offer both options, as shown below.

Overhaul Method

Large projects that need to be completed within a short period of time.

Examples:

  • An entire house organized in 6 consecutive days.
  • An entire apartment organized in a weekend.

Gradual Method

Projects where you want changes to happen gradually, rather than all at once.

Example: One 6-hour organizing session a week, spread across 2 months.
